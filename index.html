How it works

Four steps. The last one repeats.

Set up the video once, the way you want it. After that, every new video is a new script or new footage and one press of New Video.

  1. 1

    Choose the type

    A talking-head short, a product demo, an AI avatar, clips from a webinar, a story from your trip, or describe something else.

  2. 2

    Upload your files

    Recordings, screen captures or photos. Or nothing at all, if an avatar presents it.

  3. 3

    Answer the questions

    A short chat asks only what it needs: vertical or landscape, how long, who is on screen, and where to stop for you to check.

  4. 4

    Reuse the style

    Your answers become a project. Next time, press New Video, add the new script or footage, and get the same look again.

Step four

Set it up once. Reuse it every time.

Your project is a reusable video template. It remembers everything you answered: the shape, the pacing, the captions, the graphics, your brand, and where it stops for you to check. The snowboard and turkey videos came from the same project. Only the script changed.

  • A new video takes a new script, new footage or a new topic
  • Change the project once and every later video gets the change
  • Make as many videos from one project as you like
