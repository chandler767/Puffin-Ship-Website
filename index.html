How it works

From raw footage to shorts you can post

No editor to brief and no timeline to scrub. Upload what you recorded, pick the moments, and get shorts back.

01

Upload your footage

A webinar, a demo, a livestream, or any raw footage. Drop in the video file you already have.

02

It finds the moments worth posting

Puffin Ship reads what was said and what was on screen, then suggests the moments that stand on their own. Pick the ones you want.

03

Get shorts ready to post

Each moment you pick becomes its own short: cropped for vertical, captioned, pauses cut, in your brand. Set up a template once to combine moments from different recordings with a new intro recorded on camera. Review, then post.

Works on almost any footage. Runs can also start from your own tools through the API, or from AI agents like Claude Code.

Questions

Common questions

What does Puffin Ship do?

It turns any raw footage you record, from webinars and product demos to interviews, into shorts that are ready to post. It finds the moments worth posting, crops them to vertical, adds captions, cuts the pauses, and applies your brand. With a template you set up once, it can also combine moments from several recordings, with a new intro you record on camera, into one short.

What kinds of recordings work?

Almost anything you can record: webinars, product demos, livestreams, podcasts, interviews, talking-head videos, educational shorts, and gameplay and screen recordings with commentary. Upload the video file you already have.

How much does it cost?

There is one plan, priced by what you use. New workspaces start with $25 in credit, and your bill shows what each template and teammate used. Some restrictions apply.

Does it work with our tools and AI agents?

Yes. Runs, templates, and files are all available through an API, so you can start runs from your own systems on your own schedule. Claude Code, Claude Desktop, and any other MCP client can drive it directly, and can even write your templates for you.

What happens to my footage?

It stays yours. You keep ownership of everything you upload, and it is used to make your videos. Runs you rate can also help improve Puffin Ship's models; that is on by default, and a workspace admin can turn it off at any time in workspace settings.
