Questions

Common questions

What does Puffin Ship do?

It turns long recordings, such as webinars, product demos, livestreams and podcast episodes, into short clips that are ready to post. It finds the moments worth sharing, crops them to vertical, adds captions, trims dead air and applies your brand.

What kinds of recordings work?

Almost anything you can record: webinars, product demos, livestreams, podcasts, interviews, talking-head videos, educational shorts, gameplay and screen recordings. Upload the video file you already have.

How do the clips stay on brand?

You set up a template once with your fonts, colors, caption style and layout. Every clip made from it follows the same rules, so the hundredth clip looks like the first.

Can I review clips before they go out?

Yes. A run can stop and wait for your review before it finishes, and nothing is posted for you. You decide what goes out and where.

How much does it cost?

There is one plan, priced by what you use. New accounts start with credit included, and your bill shows what each template and teammate used.

Can you set it up for us?

Yes, as much as you need. We can build your templates, fit Puffin Ship into how your team already works, or run it for you and hand over finished clips. Tell us what you need using the form below.

Does it work with our tools and AI agents?

Yes. Runs, templates and files are all available through an API, so you can start runs from your own systems on your own schedule. AI assistants such as Claude can drive it directly over MCP.
