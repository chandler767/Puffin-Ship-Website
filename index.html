Questions

Common questions

What does Puffin Ship do?

It turns the video you record, such as webinars, product demos, interviews and talking-head videos, into short clips that are ready to post. It finds the moments worth sharing, crops them to vertical, adds captions, trims dead air and applies your brand. With a template you set up once, it can also combine moments you describe from several recordings, with a new intro you record on camera, into one short.

What kinds of recordings work?

Almost anything you can record: webinars, product demos, livestreams, podcasts, interviews, talking-head videos, educational shorts, gameplay and screen recordings with commentary. Upload the video file you already have.

Do I have to build a template first?

No. Start from a ready-made one, such as Webinar to Clips, Stream and Screen Highlights, Teleprompter Short or News Talking Head, and change it later if you want. None of them needs an account with another service.

How do the clips stay on brand?

You set up a template once with your colors, caption style and layout. Every clip made from it follows the same rules, so the hundredth clip looks like the first.

Can I review clips before they go out?

Yes. A run can stop and wait for your review before it finishes, and nothing is posted for you. You decide what goes out and where.

How much does it cost?

There is one plan, priced by what you use. New workspaces start with $25 in credit, and your bill shows what each template and teammate used. Some restrictions apply.

Can you set it up for us?

Yes, as much as you need. We can build your templates, fit Puffin Ship into how your team already works, or run it for you and hand over finished clips. Tell us what you need using the form below.

Does it work with our tools and AI agents?

Yes. Runs, templates and files are all available through an API, so you can start runs from your own systems on your own schedule. Claude Code, Claude Desktop and any other MCP client can drive it directly, and can even write your templates for you.

What happens to my footage?

It stays yours. You keep ownership of everything you upload, and it is used to make your videos. Runs you rate can also help improve Puffin Ship's models; that is on by default, and a workspace admin can turn it off at any time in workspace settings.
