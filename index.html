Questions

Common questions

What does Puffin Ship do?

We produce short form video for your brand and keep it coming. Depending on what you need, we can hand you finished videos, publish them to your channels on a schedule, or run the whole program and tune it against what performs.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on volume and how involved you want us to be, so we quote each brand individually. Tell us what you need and we'll come back with a number.

How do you produce so much video?

We built our own production platform and we run it ourselves. Our output isn't limited by how many editors we can hire.

Do I have to give you access to my accounts?

Only if you want us publishing for you. If you'd rather post everything yourself, we hand you finished video and you keep full control of your channels.

Can we use the platform ourselves?

Yes. Access to the platform and its API can be part of an engagement if your team would rather run some of it themselves. We set the access up for you; there is no public sign-up.
