How it works

From raw material to finished video, in software you run

Three steps, all in the browser. Nothing to install, no render farm to manage.

01

Connect your source material

Add clips, calls, audio, ideas, or docs. Whatever you already have becomes source material for the run.

02

AI builds your template

Your prompt becomes a reusable template. The AI generates hooks, scripts, captions, audio, and structure, filling in whatever is missing.

03

Review, then export

Pause any step to approve, edit, or regenerate its output before the final render. Then export a finished MP4, subtitle files, or a Final Cut Pro project if you want to take it further yourself.
