Common questions

Can't find yours? Email info@puffinship.com or ask for help setting it up.

What does Puffin Ship do?

It makes videos for you. Choose the type of video, upload your files and answer a few questions, and it builds a project that does the editing: it picks the best moments from all your recordings, cuts the pauses, frames the shot for any screen, and adds captions, b-roll, music, animated graphics and your brand. It can also write a script and put it on a teleprompter while you record, add AI voices and avatars, and bring in generated or stock footage. You get back a finished video, or a Final Cut Pro project if you want to keep editing. Every video after that reuses the same project, whether you start it in the app, through the API, or from your AI agent.

What can I make with it?

Talking-head shorts, shorts from a script you write, AI avatar videos, product demos and walkthroughs, tutorials, clips from a webinar or podcast, stream and gameplay highlights, a news or commentary series, stories from your own footage, founder and pitch videos, animated explainers, and shorts from a blog post. If your idea is none of these, describe it and Puffin Ship builds the project with you.

Do I have to set it up again for every video?

No. Your answers become a project, a reusable video template. For the next video, press New Video and add this time's script or footage, and it comes out in the same style. Change the project once and every later video gets the change.

What kinds of recordings work?

Almost anything you can record: webinars, product demos, livestreams, podcasts, interviews, talking-head videos, educational shorts, and gameplay and screen recordings with commentary. Upload the video file you already have. Some types need no footage at all, such as an AI avatar reading your script.

Do I need to know how to edit or code?

No. Choose the type, upload your files and answer a few questions in plain words. You can even ask for a moment by describing it, like "the part where we explain pricing."

Can I use it from my AI agent?

Yes. Puffin Ship works with any AI tool that supports MCP, including ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Gemini CLI and VS Code. Add https://app.puffinship.com/mcp as a connector, sign in, and ask for the video you want. It asks before anything that costs money. Developers can also use the API to start videos from their own systems.

How much does it cost?

There is one plan, priced by what you use. A token is one cent, and a typical short uses about 100 to 350 tokens. New accounts start with $25 in credit, and your bill shows what each project and teammate used. Some restrictions apply.

What happens to my footage?

It stays yours. You keep ownership of everything you upload, and it is used to make your videos. Versions you rate can also help improve Puffin Ship's models; that is on by default, and a workspace admin can turn it off at any time in workspace settings.
