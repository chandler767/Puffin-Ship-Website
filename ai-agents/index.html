What it can do

Everything you do in the browser, from the chat

A connected agent works in your account as you, with your role in each workspace.

Start a project with you

The same four steps as the app: it asks what type of video you want, works through your files and settings, and stops once the project is ready.

Get your footage in

It gives you an upload link to drop your files on, or sends them itself from a coding tool on your computer.

Make and follow the video

It starts a version once you agree, follows it to the end, and answers each review stop the way you decide.

Hand you the files

Download links for the MP4, the subtitles and the Final Cut Pro project, and a share link others can comment on.

Fix what you point at

Leave a comment at a moment of the video, fix a caption, and carry the fix back to the project for every later video.

Check before it hands over

It reads a health check of the cut and looks at frames from the video before telling you it is ready.

Built to be careful

You stay in charge

It asks before it spends

Connecting costs nothing, and neither does reading, uploading or setting up. It asks before every video it makes, and one yes covers one version.

Keys never go in the chat

When a step needs a key for another service, it gives you a link to a Puffin Ship page to paste it into. Your agent never sees it.

Your account stays yours

Your password, payments and account settings stay in the web app, where only you can change them.
