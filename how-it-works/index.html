How it works

Four steps to your first video. One click to the next.

You never touch a timeline. You say what you want, Puffin Ship builds a project that makes it, and the project makes every video after it the same way.

  1. 1

    Choose the type

    A talking-head short, a product demo, an AI avatar, clips from a webinar, a story from your trip, or describe something else.

  2. 2

    Upload your files

    Recordings, screen captures or photos. Or nothing at all, if an avatar presents it.

  3. 3

    Answer the questions

    A short chat asks only what it needs: vertical or landscape, how long, who is on screen, and where to stop for you to check.

  4. 4

    Reuse the style

    Your answers become a project. Next time, press New Video, add the new script or footage, and get the same look again.

Reuse the style

Your answers become a project: the recipe for this kind of video. When it says it is ready, press New Video, add this time's script or footage, and the video comes out in the same style.

Every type you can start from

These are the cards on the New project screen. Each one starts from a ready-made project you can change.

  • 🎙️Talking-head short from a recordingOne video of you talking, tightened into a captioned short.
  • 📝Short from a script you writeYou write the words; read them on camera or have an avatar present them.
  • 🧑‍💻AI avatar presenterAn avatar presents your script, with captions and graphics.
  • 🖥️Product demo or walkthroughA screen recording of your product, narrated or presented, kept in step with the voice.
  • 🛠️Tutorial for a tool or libraryA screen or terminal recording with a presenter explaining each step.
  • ✂️Clips from a webinar, talk or podcastOne long recording cut into several short clips worth sharing.
  • 🎮Stream or gameplay highlightsThe best moments of a stream, game or screen session, cut into highlights.
  • 📰News or commentary seriesA recurring series that finds fresh topics each time and explains one.
  • 🎞️Story from your footageYour own clips from a trip, event or day cut into one short story.
  • 🚀Founder or pitch videoThe short founder introduction an accelerator or investor asks for.
  • ✨Explainer told in animated graphicsAn idea explained mostly in animated graphics, with a presenter for a few beats.
  • 📄Turn a blog post or article into a shortAn article or post you wrote, turned into a short video.
  • 💬Something elseDescribe what you want to make and the assistant builds it with you.
After the first video

Change anything without starting over

Edit one video

Trim a clip, move it, or fix a caption word in the Edits panel of any version. Like the fix? Carry it back to the project so every later video gets it too.

It stops when you want to check

Choose the steps a video pauses at for you: after the script is written, at the storyboard, or before anything paid is made.

Take it anywhere

Download the MP4, the subtitles, and a Final Cut Pro project if you want to keep editing by hand. Share a link others can comment on.

Your brand on every video

Set up a brand kit once, with your colors, fonts and logo, and every video from the project wears it.

Ask the project assistant

Ask how something works or ask for a change. Changes come back as a proposal you read first, and nothing changes until you press Apply.
