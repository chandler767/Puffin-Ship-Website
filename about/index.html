About

Video is where the attention went. Making it stayed slow.

Puffin Ship is automated video production: you say what you want once, and it makes that kind of video for you from then on.

Why now

Everyone records hours of video. Editing it is still done by hand.

Short video is where people pay attention, and every team and creator is asked for more of it. AI can now understand footage well enough to do the editing, and to do it the same way every time.

Who's building it

Hi, I'm Chandler Mayo.

I've built software for more than a decade and spent the last seven years in developer relations at Redpanda, Doppler, and PubNub, making talks, videos, and technical content. Turning those recordings into shorts was always the slow part, so I built Puffin Ship to do it. I've been running it to make shorts for early teams, and everything it does for them, you can do yourself.
